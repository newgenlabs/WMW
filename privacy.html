Privacy and security

How your information is protected

Encrypted connection

This site is served only over HTTPS, so everything between your browser and the site is encrypted in transit.

Quote requests are encrypted end to end

When you send a quote request, your name, phone, email, ZIP or postcode, project details and saved selections are encrypted inside your browser before they leave your device, using AES-256-GCM with a key wrapped by RSA-OAEP. Only Whitestone holds the private key that can open the message. Hosting providers, networks and email services only see unreadable ciphertext.

What is stored on your device

Your saved stone selections and a small receipt (date and request type) are kept in your browser's local storage so you can return to them. No personal contact details are stored. You can remove them at any time from the page, or by clearing your browser data.

What is not collected

This site uses no advertising trackers, no analytics cookies and no account logins. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads.

Security contact

To report a security concern, email the address in the footer of the home page.
